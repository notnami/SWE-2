Welcome to MyFitnessBud!

Your fitness and nutrition companion.

MyFitnessBud is an attempt by our group to emulate the experience of MyFitnessPal. We've implemented some of the most prominent functionality, including tools to track calorie intake and workout routines. Make an account, sign in, and click the links in the navbar above to try them out!

User Profile

Guide
  • "Workouts" is where you can track your regular workout routines. Keep track of them, save them to your account, and stay organized (and motivated)!

  • "Calories" is where you can keep close tabs on your calorie intake and the calorie totals of each snack in your diet. The list includes a total number of calories, helpful for monitoring your daily nutrition!

  • "Snacks" is where you can easily find and add the snacks themselves. You can search, filter by allergens, and mark certain snacks as 'favorites' (more on that below).

  • "Favorites" is where you will be able to view your favorite snacks in their own separate list.

  • And finally, "Login/Logout" will take you to the login page. Here, you can enter your account information or sign up with a new combination if this is your first visit.
